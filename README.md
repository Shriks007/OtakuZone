# OtakuZone ⛩️

> **OtakuZone** is an all-in-one Anime & Manga Android application dedicated to watching, reading, and tracking your favorite series seamlessly.

---

## 🌟 Key Features (Planned)

- **📺 Anime Streaming & Watching**:
  - Discover trending, popular, and seasonal anime.
  - Video player with episode navigation, subtitle selection, and quality switching.
  - Episode watch progress synchronization.

- **📖 Manga Reader**:
  - Browse manga, manhwa, and manhua.
  - Reader modes: Webtoon (vertical scroll) and standard page-turn.
  - Chapter bookmarks and reading history.

- **📊 Comprehensive Tracking**:
  - Sync watchlist & readlist (Watching, Completed, Plan to Watch, Dropped, On Hold).
  - Integration support for services like AniList, MyAnimeList, and Kitsu.
  - Airing schedules and notification reminders for upcoming episodes & chapters.

- **🎨 Modern Otaku UI/UX**:
  - Sleek dark theme with customizable accent colors.
  - Rich anime/manga details: synopsis, genres, voice actors, character profiles, trailers, and user reviews.
